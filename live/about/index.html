About Bapa's Diary

What this is

Bapa's Diary is a small offering of love, so that we can stay close to Pramukh Swami Maharaj and Mahant Swami Maharaj every single day.

Here you can relive Swamishri's divine darshan, day by day, through the years: his gentle smile, his blessings, the villages and cities he sanctified on his vicharan, and the utsavs of the BAPS calendar. Pick any date to have darshan from that very day. "On this day" brings together every memory of the same date across the years, so each day begins with fresh darshan and fresh inspiration.
